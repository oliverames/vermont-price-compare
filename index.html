    How these prices work

    A shared code does not always mean the same bill. The site keeps settings, billing classes, modifiers, and price types separate.

    Discounted cash
    The provider's published cash or self-pay amount. Separate clinician, anesthesia, pathology, or facility bills may still apply.
    Gross charge
    The provider's published list or gross charge before insurance discounts, financial assistance, or other reductions.
    Negotiated range
    The lowest and highest dollar rates across published health-plan contracts. The site does not identify individual plans.
    Other published rate
    Independent facilities may publish category ceilings, comparison rates, or examples. The site keeps each source label separate.
    Your estimate
    Confirm the code with the provider, then ask the provider and your health plan for a patient-specific estimate.

    Method and limits

    Sources and limits

    The site processes every available hospital machine-readable file in scope and verified price lists from audited independent facilities. Federal transparency rules apply to hospitals, not every independent clinician or outpatient center.

    • Vermont Department of Health Hospital Report Cards defines the Vermont hospital inventory.
    • CMS Hospital Price Transparency explains the hospital publication requirement and each price type.
    • Provider source links, declared dates, and catalogue status appear in the coverage ledger above.

    A published price does not guarantee your out-of-pocket cost. Emergency care decisions should never depend on this site.
