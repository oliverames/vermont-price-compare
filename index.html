    How These Prices Work

    A CPT code identifies a specific medical service. Matching the code and price type makes the comparison useful.

    Total Gross Charge
    The hospital and hospital-employed physician charges shown in Vermont's 2026 report. Insurance discounts are not applied.
    Discounted Cash Price
    A provider's published cash amount for the coded service. The file may not identify every billing component, and another provider may bill separately.
    Your Estimate
    Ask the provider and your health plan for an estimate that uses your exact CPT code, network, deductible, and coinsurance.

    Sources and Limits

    The five examples use public files that were available on August 5, 2026. This demo does not include every Vermont clinician, independent imaging center, or health plan rate.

    • Vermont Department of Health Hospital Report Cards for statewide CPT gross charges.
    • Provider Price Transparency Files for selected discounted cash prices.
    • Green Mountain Surgery Center Billing Information for separate facility charges.
    • CMS Hospital Price Transparency Guidance for an explanation of published hospital charges.
